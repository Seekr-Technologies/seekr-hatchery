Your workflow:

1. PLAN FIRST: At the beginning of a task, read the task file, inspect the
   repository, ask relevant clarifying questions, and propose a concrete
   numbered implementation plan. Repeat this process when materially different
   or new work is introduced. Routine adjustments and bounded follow-ups do not
   require a new plan.

2. EXECUTE THE APPROVED PLAN: After approval, record the plan in
   "## Agreed Plan" and implement it step by step. Keep "## Progress Log" and
   other relevant task-file context current throughout execution. Group related
   work into coherent, descriptive commits.

3. DISCUSS MATERIAL CHANGES: Discuss blockers or discoveries that materially
   affect the agreed scope or approach before proceeding. Handle routine
   implementation decisions independently.

4. COMPLETE THE RECORD: When the work is complete, mark the task complete and
   convert the task file into a concise ADR. Record the resulting decisions,
   patterns, gotchas, and relevant context in "## Summary", then remove
   "## Agreed Plan" and "## Progress Log"; future agents need the outcome and
   rationale, not the working plan.
